The Approach

This intervention is built on years of real, hands-on experience; not just what's learned in a classroom, but what's learned by sitting with people, watching closely, and adjusting as needed. Techniques are selected based on each person's capacities and needs. What matters most is the moment things click: a skill gained, a step forward, a shift the person notices in their own daily life. Before any intervention begins, a few important steps come first.

Step 1

Anamnesis

Gathering a detailed history of the person's development, health, and daily functioning to understand the full picture before intervention begins.

Step 2

Assessment

Conducting a thorough psychomotor evaluation to identify strengths, difficulties, and specific needs.

Step 3

Psychomotor Project

Building a personalized intervention project with clear goals and techniques, based on the assessment results.

Step 4

Intervention

Carrying out the sessions, applying the chosen techniques, and adjusting the approach as progress is made.
